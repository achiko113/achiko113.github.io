Guided Tours

Open a larger version of the following image in a popup:

Discover Georgian art through curated experiences at Art Foundation Anagi. Our tours offer intimate engagement with significant works from our permanent collection and rotating exhibitions, led by specialists in Georgian art history.

Each visit is thoughtfully designed to foster dialogue and reveal the historical and artistic contexts that define Georgia's unique cultural heritage.

The Experience

Your visit unfolds across multiple gallery spaces, beginning at our Art Concept Gallery on the ground floor. Tours proceed through eight distinct exhibition areas spanning three levels, including Sergo Kobuladze's reconstructed studio, contemporary installations, and our comprehensive map collection.

The standard tour concludes in our amphitheater cinema, where select screenings complement the exhibitions. Our signature "Art & Wine" experience includes a tasting of Georgian wine and traditional accompaniments.

Duration: 60–90 minutes · Languages: English, Georgian

Arrange Your Visit

For tour reservations and inquiries, our team is available to assist you. We recommend booking at least one week in advance to ensure availability.

Email: info@artfoundationanagi.com
Telephone: +995 (32) 2 29 90 29

Visitor Information

Meeting Point: All tours begin at the Art Concept Gallery reception desk. We recommend arriving 10 minutes prior to your scheduled tour time.

Accessibility: The foundation is accessible via elevator to all exhibition levels. Please inform us of any specific requirements when booking.

Modifications & Cancellations: Changes to reservations may be made up to 48 hours in advance. Please contact our team directly.
