Art Foundation Anagi

AFA – Art Foundation Anagi, based in Tbilisi, Georgia, is a private cultural initiative founded by leading professionals from across the creative industries. The foundation merges the richness of Georgia's artistic heritage with research-based and innovative curatorial practices.

AFA's mission is to foster a sustainable and dynamic creative ecosystem where tradition and experimentation coexist. Through collaborations with Georgian artists, curators, collectors, and international partners, the foundation articulates new perspectives and ensures that Georgia's cultural voice continues to resonate worldwide.
