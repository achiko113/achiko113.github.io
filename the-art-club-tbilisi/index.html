The Art Club Tbilisi

Historical record. Prices, offers, membership benefits and programme descriptions below belong to the former AFA programme. No purchases, memberships or applications are accepted here.

The Art Club Tbilisi is a society for individuals and organisations driven by a profound engagement with art. Rooted in the mission of Art Foundation Anagi, the Club cultivates a community of collectors, cultural professionals, and patrons committed to advancing Georgian art — elevating its presence in the world's leading artistic and investment circles, and reinforcing its status as both cultural heritage and creative capital.

The Art Ambassador

Annual Membership

The Art Ambassador membership is designed for those for whom art is not only a source of aesthetic enjoyment but also a strategic investment, a cultural asset, and a means of personal expression. Membership establishes a privileged partnership with the Foundation, granting access to its spaces, programs, expertise, and global networks.

Ambassadors receive full access to the Foundation's program calendar, including meetings with renowned artists, visits to private studios, panel discussions, curated film screenings, and preview exhibition viewings. The Club also provides an environment for developing meaningful personal and professional relationships — connecting members with emerging and established collectors, curators, philanthropists, and representatives from across the creative fields.

Once a year, Club members are invited to a Gala Reception celebrating shared achievements and milestones within the Foundation's community.

Together, we cultivate an ecosystem where Georgian art thrives as both cultural heritage and creative capital.

Beyond Membership

Specialised services available to Club members

Collector Advisory

For discerning private collectors, the Foundation offers a bespoke framework for professionally managing and developing collections. Members may engage in privileged collaboration with the Foundation's curators to build a collection of lasting cultural and strategic significance.

Corporate Cultural Partnerships

For organisations that understand culture as strategic capital. The Foundation provides tailored corporate experiences, access to the art world, and a curated suite of services designed for the needs of teams seeking meaningful cultural engagement.

Youth & Educational Programs

The Foundation is cultivating tomorrow's patrons, curators, and cultural thinkers. Our youth program offers immersive cultural education for young people aged 7–18, including curated film screenings, creative workshops, and monthly programs designed to develop visual literacy and critical thinking.

We believe Georgian art belongs in the world's leading artistic and investment circles — as both cultural heritage and creative capital.
