The Tbilisi Arts Club

The Tbilisi Arts Club Collector membership represents the Foundation's most intimate form of collaboration. A partnership designed for patrons who understand that building a collection of international consequence requires not only vision, but also infrastructure, discretion, and institutional expertise.

What follows is a year lived at the intersection of culture, scholarship, and access.

Download Program Brochure

What to Expect

Scholarly Curation & Management

Your collection will be treated as an evolving body of scholarship. We document provenance, track conservation needs, and begin the process of assembling a formal catalogue raisonné. You work directly with senior curators who serve as discreet advisors on every acquisition decision, providing market intelligence that exists outside public channels.

Unrestricted Access

You move through our spaces as you would your own: exhibitions, reading rooms, and archives, without limitation. When exceptional works enter our Art Fund, you are contacted first—these are museum-caliber pieces, and your access precedes any public offering.

Global Networking & Travel

Once a year, travel with a small group of fellow collectors to Venice, Basel, Frieze, or another landmark event. These trips are curated for depth, not spectacle: private studio visits, curator-led tours, and access to spaces that do not appear on public itineraries.

A Discreet Network

You become part of a discreet network of patrons, collectors, and cultural architects. Each year, the Foundation hosts a single gala—a celebration of the work we've built together.

$3,000 / year

Begin Your Consultation

This membership is a personal dialogue. We invite you to send an enquiry to begin a conversation with a senior member of our team. They will contact you to schedule your initial collection consultation and personally welcome you to the Society.
