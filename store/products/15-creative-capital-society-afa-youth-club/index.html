Educational Programs

The AFA Youth Club is built on a simple belief: that young people, given the right environment and guidance, are capable of engaging with art at the highest level. This is education in its truest form, designed to cultivate visual literacy, critical thinking, and the kind of curiosity that shapes a lifelong relationship with culture.

What follows is a year of learning to see, to question, and to create.

Download Program Brochure

What to Expect

Active Inquiry & Visual Literacy

Your child will spend time with art that demands attention. Active inquiry, close looking, meaningful questions. Guided tours through the Foundation's collection teach them how to articulate what they see and why it matters. They develop an independent eye.

Creative Practice

They'll work alongside professional artists and educators in hands-on workshops. Painting, sculpting, animation: the medium changes, but the principle remains the same. To understand art, you must also make it. To interpret the decisions of others, you must first confront the difficulty of making your own.

Cinema & Critical Thinking

They'll watch films chosen for substance. Georgian cinema, carefully selected for their age and intellectual development. Afterward, they discuss what they've seen in moderated conversations that teach them to articulate responses, challenge interpretations, and develop the vocabulary to express complex ideas.

We treat young people as the engaged audience they already are.

$200 / year

Joining the Club

The AFA Youth Club accepts students ages 7 through 16. We invite you to send an enquiry to learn more about the program or to register your child. A member of our education team will be in touch to discuss the schedule and answer any questions you may have.
